**weibo-sociolinguistics-topic-summaries** is the topic-level half of the Weibo sociolinguistics project. Each research date, one Weibo hot-search topic is selected; this dataset keeps **one row per date** with the topic, the metrics Weibo displayed for it, a **model-generated, de-identified summary** of the discussion, and **aggregate counts** from that day's post labeling. It deliberately publishes **no individual post text, usernames, or per-post diagnostics**.

Its post-level companion is [weibo sociolinguistics labeled posts](/tech/datasets/weibo-labeled-posts), and human review lives separately in [weekly label reviews](/tech/datasets/weibo-weekly-label-reviews).

<Heading level={2}>1. at a glance</Heading>

- **Source**: one selected Weibo hot-search topic per research date.
- **Cadence**: daily, append-only by research date (first research date: 2026 Sept 28).
- **Unit of analysis**: one row = one research date / topic.
- **Language**: Chinese.
- **Format**: a single cumulative CSV, `data/topic_summaries.csv`, 24 columns, one `train` split.
- **License**: CC BY 4.0 for the publisher-controlled summaries, annotations, documentation, and database arrangement (see [license](#license)).

<Heading level={2}>2. how it is built</Heading>

1. **Record the hot-search list** for the day and capture topic metrics.
2. **Select one research topic.**
3. **Collect posts.** Weibo's *popular-post* results are traversed in page order, up to 200 entries before deduplication. The research date identifies the batch; it does not filter posts by publication time.
4. **Summarize.** A language model writes a topic-level account from up to 20 high-interaction posts plus up to 20 longer posts (no duplicates).
5. **Label posts** in a separate daily run; only the aggregate counts land in this dataset.
6. **De-identify and publish.** Narrative fields go through a deterministic public-text projection that replaces phone numbers, ID numbers, emails, URLs, Weibo handles, and paired hashtags with placeholders.

<Heading level={2}>3. what's in each row</Heading>

<div class="table-wrap">

| field | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `topic_key` | Stable key, `YYYY-MM-DD::topic`. |
| `date` | Research date. |
| `topic` | The selected Weibo topic text. |
| `selected_at` | UTC time the selection was recorded. |
| `url` | Source URL of the topic. |
| `read_count` · `discuss_count` · `interact_count` · `original_count` | Weibo's displayed topic metrics at capture time. |
| `description` | Weibo's own topic classification (not a generated description). |
| `metrics_captured_at` | UTC time of the metrics snapshot. |
| `summary` | Model-generated topic summary, after de-identification. |
| `summary_generated_at` · `summary_model` · `summary_prompt_version` · `summary_sampling_version` | Generation metadata. |
| `engagement_sample_count` · `longest_text_sample_count` | How many posts fed the summary. |
| `labeling_input_count` · `labeling_pass_count` · `labeling_cleaned_count` · `labeling_discarded_count` · `labeling_final_count` | Aggregate counts from the daily labeling run (`final = pass + cleaned`). |

<Heading level={2}>4. limitations & responsible use</Heading>

<Callout type="conclusion" title="Read before using">
  - **One topic per day**, chosen from hot searches, so this is not a probability sample of Weibo.
  - **"Popular" is Weibo's own ordering** at collection time, not a global ranking computed by the researcher. Timing, deletions, and deduplication all introduce bias.
  - **Summaries are model-generated** and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or culturally narrow.
  - **De-identification reduces risk but does not guarantee anonymity**: homophones, emoji spellings, text inside images, and distinctive public events can slip through. Please don't use it to identify, profile, or contact individuals.
</Callout>
