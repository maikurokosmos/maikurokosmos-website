**weibo-sociolinguistics-labeled-posts** is the post-level half of the Weibo sociolinguistics project. For each research date, the popular posts collected under that day's topic (up to 200 before deduplication) are labeled by a daily model run and released here, one row per post. The topic itself, with its summary and aggregate counts, lives in [topic summaries](/tech/datasets/weibo-topic-summaries).

</p>

<Heading level={2}>1. at a glance</Heading>

- **Source**: Weibo popular-post results for one selected hot-search topic per research date.
- **Cadence**: daily (first research date: 2026 Sept 28).
- **Size**: about 8,500 released rows so far, and counting.
- **Unit of analysis**: one row = one post, keyed by `record_key` (`YYYY-MM-DD::topic::NNN`).
- **Labels**: model-generated, with deterministic validation and cleaning. Human review is published separately in [weekly label reviews](/tech/datasets/weibo-weekly-label-reviews) and never overwrites the daily release.

<Heading level={2}>2. labels</Heading>

Each post carries the structured labels below. The value sets follow the codebook documented on the [earlier weibo corpus page](/tech/datasets/weibo-sociolinguistics).

<div class="table-wrap">

| field | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `validity` | whether the post is usable, and if not, why. |
| `target_type` | what the post is mainly about, or directed at. |
| `stance` | the post's stance toward that target. |
| `emotion_category` · `emotion_subtype` | the dominant emotion and a finer emotion within it. |
| `mf_main` · `mf_direction` | the moral foundation invoked and which pole of it. |
| `satire` | whether the post is satirical or ironic. |
| `risk_score` | a deterministic score used to flag labels that may need a closer look. |

</div>

<Callout type="conclusion" title="Read before using">
  Labels are model-generated, and posts come from Weibo's own popular ordering for one topic a day, so this is not a random sample of Weibo. See the dataset card on Hugging Face for the full field list, privacy notes, and license.
</Callout>
