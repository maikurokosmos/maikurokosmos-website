The **weibo sociolinguistics project** follows one Weibo hot-search topic per research date and studies how people talk about it. Each day's run is released as **three linked datasets** on Hugging Face, so that topic-level context, post-level labels, and human review can each be used on their own or joined together.

<Heading level={2}>1. at a glance</Heading>

- **Source**: one selected Weibo hot-search topic per research date, with up to 200 of Weibo's popular posts under it.
- **Cadence**: daily (first research date: 2026 Sept 28). Human review runs weekly.
- **Size**: about 8,500 labeled posts so far, and counting.
- **Language**: Chinese.
- **Join key**: `record_key` (`YYYY-MM-DD::topic::NNN`) for posts, `topic_key` (`YYYY-MM-DD::topic`) for topics.

<Heading level={2}>2. how a day's run works</Heading>

1. **Record the hot-search list** and capture topic metrics.
2. **Select one research topic.**
3. **Collect posts.** Weibo's *popular-post* results are traversed in page order, up to 200 entries before deduplication. The research date identifies the batch; it does not filter posts by publication time.
4. **Summarize the topic.** A language model writes a topic-level account from up to 20 high-interaction posts plus up to 20 longer posts.
5. **Label every post** in a separate model run, with deterministic validation and cleaning.
6. **De-identify and publish.** Narrative fields go through a deterministic public-text projection that replaces phone numbers, ID numbers, emails, URLs, Weibo handles, and paired hashtags with placeholders.
7. **Review weekly.** A sample of the labels is checked by hand and published separately.

<Heading level={2}>3. the three datasets</Heading>

<Heading level={3}>topic summaries</Heading>

One row per research date: the selected topic, the metrics Weibo displayed for it, a model-generated and de-identified summary, and aggregate counts from that day's labeling. It publishes **no individual post text, usernames, or per-post diagnostics**. Released as a single cumulative CSV under **CC BY 4.0**.

<div class="table-wrap">

| field | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `topic_key` · `date` · `topic` · `url` | Topic identity, research date, and source URL. |
| `read_count` · `discuss_count` · `interact_count` · `original_count` | Weibo's displayed topic metrics at capture time. |
| `description` | Weibo's own topic classification (not a generated description). |
| `summary` | Model-generated topic summary, after de-identification. |
| `summary_model` · `summary_prompt_version` · `summary_sampling_version` | Generation metadata. |
| `labeling_input_count` … `labeling_final_count` | Aggregate counts from the daily labeling run (`final = pass + cleaned`). |
| `labeling_discard_reason_counts_json` | JSON map of discard reasons to counts. |

</div>

<Heading level={3}>labeled posts</Heading>

One row per post, keyed by `record_key`. Each post carries the structured labels below; the value sets follow the codebook on the [earlier weibo corpus page](/tech/datasets/weibo-sociolinguistics).

<div class="table-wrap">

| field | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `validity` | whether the post is usable, and if not, why. |
| `target_type` | what the post is mainly about, or directed at. |
| `stance` | the post's stance toward that target. |
| `emotion_category` · `emotion_subtype` | the dominant emotion and a finer emotion within it. |
| `mf_main` · `mf_direction` | the moral foundation invoked and which pole of it. |
| `satire` | whether the post is satirical or ironic. |
| `risk_score` | a deterministic score used to flag labels that may need a closer look. |

</div>

<Heading level={3}>weekly label reviews</Heading>

Once a week, a sample of the daily labels is reviewed by hand and the verdicts are published here. The daily release is append-only, so reviews never rewrite it; keeping them apart means the model's labels can always be compared with the reviewed ones, which is how labeling quality is measured.

<Heading level={2}>4. limitations & responsible use</Heading>

<Callout type="conclusion" title="Read before using">
  - **One topic per day**, chosen from hot searches, so this is not a probability sample of Weibo.
  - **"Popular" is Weibo's own ordering** at collection time, not a global ranking computed by the researcher. Timing, deletions, and deduplication all introduce bias.
  - **Summaries and daily labels are model-generated.** Use the weekly reviews when you need human-checked labels.
  - **De-identification reduces risk but does not guarantee anonymity**: homophones, emoji spellings, text inside images, and distinctive public events can slip through. Please don't use the data to identify, profile, or contact individuals.
</Callout>

Questions and removal requests go through each dataset's Discussions on Hugging Face. Please share only the affected `record_key` (or date and topic), never identity documents or other sensitive evidence. You can also write to **[maikurokosmos@gmail.com](mailto:maikurokosmos@gmail.com)**.
